🌿 Leafy

🎬 Try StepCast on a pretend app

Leafy isn't a real plant app. It's a small demo page for testing StepCast. Nothing here is saved, so click around freely, and a reload brings back the starter plants.

  1. Press ● Record in the StepCast panel on the right.
  2. Click around Leafy: add a plant and give it a name, water one, peek into a ⋯ menu.
  3. Press ■ Stop, then click each step to give it a friendly subtitle.
  4. Press ▶ Preview to try it, or Export and drop the file into stepcast/tutorials/.

StepCast records clicks, typing and dropdown choices. Password fields are never recorded.

How to add StepCast to your site
  1. Copy the stepcast/ folder into your project. Any folder works, just keep everything inside it together.
  2. While making tutorials, add the recorder to your page: 
    <script src="stepcast/stepcast-recorder.js"></script>
  3. On your live site, add only the player. Never the recorder. 
    <script src="stepcast/stepcast-player.js"></script>
  4. Start a tutorial with a link: 
    <a href="#" data-tutorial="add-a-plant">Show me how</a>

Serve your pages over http, not by opening the file directly. Change the paths above if you put the folder somewhere else. More in stepcast/README.md.
