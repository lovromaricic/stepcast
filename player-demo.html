How to add tutorials to your site
  1. Copy the stepcast/ folder into your project, with your tutorials in stepcast/tutorials/.
  2. Add the player to your page: 
    <script src="stepcast/stepcast-player.js"></script>
  3. Start a tutorial with a link: 
    <a href="#" data-tutorial="add-a-plant">Show me how</a>

Serve your pages over http, not by opening the file directly. More in stepcast/README.md.
